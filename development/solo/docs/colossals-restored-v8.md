# SF Test 1.7 v8 - Colossals Restored

Colossals are a base-critical SoloFoots system. They were present in the map data but disconnected in the Performance Test 6 baseline.

Version 8 restores the original SoloFoots v1.7 wiring for:

- default Colossal enablement;
- Colossal variables and stat initialization;
- initial and recurring wave timers;
- the ten-second wave warning and minimap pings;
- wave spawning and scaling;
- Colossal death handling and drops;
- the Colossal timer dialog and status display.

The original defaults are preserved: ten waves, first-wave delay of 150 seconds, recurring delay of 300 seconds, and four base-adjacent spawn points plus the center Colossal.
