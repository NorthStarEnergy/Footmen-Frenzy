# Poker

Play at the open table or against the house. The archive also includes the original observer-poker prototype.

[799X](799x/) · [Solo Foots](solo/) · [FFA Foots](ffa/)

799X has separate [open-table](799x/open-table.j) and [house-table](799x/house-table.j) code. Solo Foots supports a second player or practice against the computer. FFA Foots has a house opponent. The [observer-poker prototype](../Shared%20Casino/Development%20history/observer-poker/) includes its build scripts, artwork tools and rule tests. Poker and blackjack share the [card and chip textures](../Shared%20Casino/Assets/PokerUI/).
