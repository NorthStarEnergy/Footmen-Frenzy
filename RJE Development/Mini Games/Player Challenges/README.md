# Player Challenges

Two players take opposite sides on a predicted match winner. An accepted challenge holds equal stakes until the result.

[799X](799x/) · [Solo Foots](solo/) · [FFA Foots](ffa/)

Each family folder holds the offer, acceptance, cancellation and settlement functions as well as the challenge screen. The [prototype rules](../Shared%20Casino/Development%20history/casino-v1/#player-challenges) describe who backs each outcome and when stakes are refunded.
