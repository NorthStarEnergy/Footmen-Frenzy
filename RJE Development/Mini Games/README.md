# Mini Games

| Game | What you play |
|---|---|
| [Poker](Poker/) | Play at the open table or against the house. The archive also includes the original observer-poker prototype. |
| [Blackjack](Blackjack/) | Play against the dealer, hit, stand, double or split. The spectator controls let another player follow a hand. |
| [Match Betting](Match%20Betting/) | Buy a ticket on the first hero kill, a base kill, the first base lost, the first to level 16 or the match winner. Each ticket keeps the odds quoted at purchase. |
| [Player Challenges](Player%20Challenges/) | Two players take opposite sides on a predicted match winner. An accepted challenge holds equal stakes until the result. |
| [Arena 1v1](Arena%201v1/) | Accept a challenge and fight with a hero and 40 footmen per side in a separate arena. The 799X version supports free duels and matched wallet stakes. |

The [shared casino files](Shared%20Casino/) contain the menu, wallet, artwork and early prototypes. These are archived source extracts for 799X, Solo Foots and FFA Foots.
