Unit balance

Banshee projectile speed is 1600, Archer 1750, Wraith 1700 and Kodo Beast 1300. Kodo starts with 0 armor and 700 HP in 799X or 750 HP in Solo and FFA. Ghouls attack 5% faster. Witch Doctor has Healing Ward, Serpent Ward, Sentry Ward and Transmute.

Hero rotation

Mundo, Demon Knight, Veigar and Twisted Fate are retired in all three builds, including All Random and the -tf command. Their implementations are archived in RJE Development.

Witch Doctor

Sentry Ward is visible, retains True Sight, and has 50, 100, 125, 175, 200 and 250 HP at ranks 1 through 6.
