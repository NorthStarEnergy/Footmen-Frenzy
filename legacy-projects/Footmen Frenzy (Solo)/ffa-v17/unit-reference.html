Solo Foots v17 — unit reference

v17 • Overdrive research: 250 gold. All five races: five tiers each, plus shared starting Footmen. Values are unupgraded troop stats, before auras, items, research, buffs and armor mitigation. Range/movement/projectile values are Warcraft world units; movement/projectiles are per second. Turn rate is the raw Warcraft object field, not degrees/second. N/A projectiles mean melee/instant delivery. Raw DPS is average primary attack damage divided by cooldown, before armor, animation delays or splash.

Steady production is one unit per wave; Overdrive produces two. Shared timer first fires after 5.416667 seconds, then every 6.666667. Other active production timers are 5 and 7.5 seconds and become enabled after mass ends. Startup initializes the wave count to two; it returns to one after an Overdrive expiration. The cap check uses ≤50 living units of the current troop type, so a wave can briefly exceed 50. The unused unit_spawnrate array is excluded.

Audit findings: v14's 20% spawn increase changed only the shared timer, not the 5/7.5-second timers. Same-tier swaps can therefore change a unit's spawn interval: the complete swap table is below. Overdrive's activation tooltip says 45 seconds/80-second cooldown, research tooltip says 60/90, while the script tracks 45/110. These existing discrepancies are reported here; v17 changes only its research price. T1–T3 native XP levels 2/3 give 38/57, plus any eligible 95%-scaled scripted bonus. Footmen are level 1 (25 native XP) and receive separate scripted bonuses; a timed trigger can later change their level.
