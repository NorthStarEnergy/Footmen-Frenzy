## Script details and limits

Forked Fury: source.j:50325–50402; 4 accepted rolls out of 16, attack-start event, structure/magic-immune filters, 10-second manually started cooldown. ACfl:250 damage,8 targets,900 cast range. Frost orb:source.j:50404–50429, B00L triggers A06R, zero damage,175 AoE,1-second duration. Demogorgon:source.j:50714–50739, A08I triggers A08H at target point; A08H level1 data has17 full damage/0.1s and10 residual damage/0.25s. No ability level4 assignment occurs. Avoid claiming level4 or exact integrated420 damage without engine playtesting of Flame Strike phase timing.

Custom ability copies resolve defaults from their original game object, not the map-modified original. custom_v1 gives AIcb armor reduction5; the earlier4 correction was wrong. Statistical bonuses from attributes are additional and hero-dependent. Ability stacking, actual slow percentages from engine gameplay constants, spell resistance, actual cast success/dummy lifetime, and Flame Strike phase timing need engine interpretation; raw values are retained in the JSON. This is a complete static trace of all 19 item combination triggers, not a playtest.
