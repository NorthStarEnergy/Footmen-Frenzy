Mini Foots v4 — unit and request audit

Extracted from the combined October 28 build. Map object overrides are resolved against the installed Warcraft III base, custom_v1 balance, and English profile data. Script values are traced through initialization and the active scheduler. This is a source/archive audit; the combined build has not been tested in a new match.

“—” / “-” means an unset source field; “unresolved” means no inherited field was found. Neither is silently converted to zero. Times are seconds; speeds are Warcraft units/second. Object gold/build times describe the unit template, not a gold charge for automatic spawning.

Findings

  • Eleven distinct army units are reachable through 21 retained base entries. Both Burning Archer paths now spawn the modified Ghoul (675 HP), and both T1 Headhunter paths spawn the modified Grunt (800 HP). Both use normal attacks and heavy armor. Huntress/Banshee/Wraith are unarmored; Militia is heavy with 900 HP.
  • The active spawn intervals are 4.5, 5, 6, 6.5, 7 and 8 seconds. All are exact multiples of the 0.25-second scheduler tick.
  • Normal post-mass production is one unit per wave. Opening footmen use two per regular wave, then the Mini Foots 1.5× multiplier; opening bonus waves are multiplied too. Teammates round complementarily, preserving the full three-player team total.
  • Production requires an active player, a living matching base, production enabled and the phase unlocked. The timer is free-running: an upgrade can catch the next scheduled wave. A wave is allowed at exactly 60 living units of its type, so a normal wave can reach 61; this inherited check is not a strict cap at 60.
  • The old level-two comment says eight minutes, but the actual level trigger fires at 600 seconds (ten minutes). Crypt Fiends are assigned level 3 then overridden to level 2 by the scheduler behavior table.
  • Hardened Skin A0A0/A0A2/A0BZ has zero chance, zero reduction and both melee/ranged inclusion disabled. It currently contributes no protection.
  • At the 2820-second bounty timer, only the original index set is updated; duplicate alternate tech entries retain their initial bounty. Both values are tabulated below.
  • Some abilities retain research prerequisites, notably Robk, Rhsb and Rotr. Their presence in an ability list does not prove they are usable: those prerequisites are not offered by the remaining attack/armor-only base research menus.

Script rules applied on spawn

HP multiplier is 1.00. Attack upgrade level L (0–5) adds L damage dice: damage = base + (dice + L)d(sides). Armor upgrades add 2 per level, up to +10. The disabled special research branches no longer add HP, damage or range. Both object weapon ranges are zero; the compensated native setter applies the requested runtime range without accumulating it at mass release.

Footmen receive +30% movement speed for six seconds when released into mass (310 → 403). Their opening staging temporarily hides, pauses and protects them and disables pathing/food. A seasonal flag can add A0DD to eligible indices; this is conditional and is documented separately below.
